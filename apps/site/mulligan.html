How it works

From topped shot back to the tee in a minute.

Juniors top a lot of shots, and most of them stop within twenty metres. Mulligan turns those into extra swings instead of a wasted bucket.

  1. 1

    Book a bay, add the robot

    Pick a time and a bay in the app, tick the robot, and pay for a bucket. Mulligan is waiting on its charger when you arrive.

  2. 2

    Drive out and scoop

    One thumb on the stick. Roll over a ball and it pops through the soft fingers into the corral. It holds ten.

  3. 3

    Bring them home, hit again

    Drive back to the mat, lift the balls out and tee them up. Mum and Dad's bucket stays full.

Games

Turn a bucket of balls into a game.

Pick a club, drop a target ring on a map of the range, and tap where each ball lands. The app keeps score, so every bucket has something to beat.

  • Bullseye

    Drop the ring anywhere on the range and tap where each ball lands. The app counts how many found the target.

  • Shrink the Ring

    Three in a row and the ring gets smaller. See how tight you can get it before the bucket runs out.

  • Beat Your Best

    Every round is saved by club, so you can watch your accuracy climb from one visit to the next.

And when one dribbles off the tee, Mulligan fetches it for another try.

Safety

Made for a range with people hitting on it.

The robot goes out so that children never have to.

  • Hold to drive

    It moves only while a thumb is on the stick. Let go, or lose signal, and it stops.

  • Stays in its lane

    A limit keeps it within 30 m and in front of its own bay, away from other golfers' shots.

  • Takes a direct hit

    A low, sloped shell sheds a stray ball and shields the battery.

  • Nothing spins

    Balls push through soft fingers into the corral. There are no rollers or blades to catch small hands.

For range owners

What it does for the range.

Mulligan makes a family hour at the range go further, without changing how the range runs.

  • Nobody walks onto the grass

    The robot fetches the short ones, so kids stay behind the line.

  • Families stay for the whole bucket

    Topped shots come back for another go instead of ending the fun early.

  • A reason to come back

    Target games and saved scores give juniors something to beat on the next visit.

  • Bays booked and paid up front

    Bookings and bucket sales run through one app, with a code for the ball machine.

  • A new add-on to sell

    Robot hire goes onto a bay booking with one tap.

  • Nothing to rebuild

    It works from the bays and mats you already have, and parks on a charger at closing time.

We're looking for our first range.

Mulligan is at prototype stage. We'd like to run a pilot on a couple of bays at one range, and shape the booking side around how that range really works.
