# RangeOps name check

Checked 2026-11-19.

**Summary.** No one in Australia has a trade mark, application, company or registered business name for RANGEOPS or RANGE OPS. That covers all classes, not just 9, 28, 41 and 42. The closest Australian marks are other driving-range brands built on the word RANGE: RANGE SERVANT, TOPTRACER RANGE, Toro's RANGE PRO ball collector and LIV Golf's RANGEGOATS GC. None is close enough to block RangeOps. The one exact match anywhere is a US registration, RANGE OPS (Reg. 6242590). It belongs to a Utah firearms-training business and covers class 45 (personal security education), so it doesn't reach golf software and has no effect in Australia. In practice, three things count against the name. First, `rangeops.com` and `rangeops.io` are owned by someone else and parked. Second, an iPhone app called **"RangeOps: Firearm Range Log"** has been on the App Store since October 2026. Third, "range ops" is close to descriptive for driving-range operations software, which makes it a weaker mark that is harder to enforce. `rangeops.com.au`, `rangeops.golf` and `rangeopsgolf.com` are unregistered. `rangeops.app` is in the registry's pending-delete stage. **Verdict: clear to proceed in Australia, with the caveats below.**

> This is an informal search by a non-lawyer, not legal advice. In IP Australia's words, results of any search "should not be relied upon", because a trade mark examiner searches again when an application is assessed ([search disclaimer](https://search.ipaustralia.gov.au/trademarks/disclaimer)). Its disclaimer also warns that lapsed or removed marks can be restored, and that its register only covers rights applied for in Australia. Australia is also a "first to use" country, so someone who uses a name without registering it can still have rights. Search engines and registers miss things.

A **.com.au** needs an Australian presence. Under auDA's [licensing rules](https://www.auda.org.au/au-domain-names/au-rules-and-policies/au-domain-administration-rules-licensing-2/) and [eligibility and allocation rules](https://files.auda.org.au/documents/Domain-Name-Eligibility-and-Allocation-Policy-Rules-July-2024.pdf), that means an ABN, an ACN, a registered business name, or being an Australian citizen or resident. An overseas owner of an Australian trade mark can also qualify, but only for an exact match of that mark. The name must also be an exact match, abbreviation or acronym of the registrant's name, or "closely and substantially connected" to it, for example a product or service the registrant provides ([auDA on using a trade mark](https://www.auda.org.au/news-insights/blog/comau-and-netau-rule-changes-explained-using-trade-mark-meet-australian-presence-requirement/)). A sole trader with an ABN can register `rangeops.com.au` for a RangeOps service they provide, and registering a RANGEOPS business name makes the case clear-cut. The presence requirement has to keep being met for as long as the licence runs.

## Recommendation for the plan

**Decision 1 in [docs/PLAN.md](../PLAN.md), "Is the name RangeOps free to use?": clear to proceed in Australia, with caveats.** Nothing on the Australian or US registers, in WIPO's database or among Australian business names blocks RangeOps for a driving-range app, booking service, games or staff console. No other golf business uses the name.

Trade-offs to accept, or to act on:

1. **It's a weak mark.** "Range" plus "ops" reads as "driving-range operations", which comes close to describing a staff console. An examiner could object in classes 9 and 42 that the mark isn't capable of distinguishing those goods and services (Trade Marks Act 1995, s 41). Even once registered, it would stop only near-identical names. The player-facing side (booking, games) is less descriptive than the console, and a logo or a "RangeOps Golf" form helps. TM Headstart (below) is a cheap way to see what an examiner thinks before paying the full fee.
2. **The .com is taken.** `rangeops.com` and `rangeops.io` are parked at GoDaddy. Use `rangeops.com.au` (needs an ABN) and `rangeops.golf`, both free today. Buying the .com from its owner is possible but the price is unknown.
3. **A firearms app shares the exact name.** On the App Store, list the app as "RangeOps Golf" or similar. If the shooting association bothers you, now is the cheapest time to rename.
4. **US use.** The Utah RANGE OPS mark covers different services, so it's a low risk. It's still worth a proper clearance before any US launch.
5. **Mulligan** is clear for a private proof of concept in Australia, with no live marks in classes 7, 9 or 28. The US class 28 is crowded with MULLIGAN golf goods, which matters only if the robot is ever sold there under that name.
